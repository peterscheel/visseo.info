04 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

01What does Visseo do?

Visseo is an AI-engineered SEO and web studio that builds websites, optimizes search and AI-search visibility (SEO & GEO), and provides AI consulting, branding, and Cloudflare-hosted backend infrastructure for businesses across BeNeLux and Germany.

02What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing content and structure so AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can find, understand and cite it — alongside traditional SEO for search engines like Google.

03Does Visseo build websites with AI?

Yes. Visseo designs and builds custom websites using Claude, with high-speed hosting and infrastructure deployed on Cloudflare's global edge network.

04Which regions does Visseo serve?

Visseo works with businesses across BeNeLux (Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg) and Germany.

05Who is behind Visseo?

Visseo is run by Peter Scheel, who has worked hands-on on production websites since being introduced to web design, SEO and hosting by his brother's agency. Visseo grew out of that experience as a side project in 2022.

06Where are Visseo websites hosted?

On Cloudflare's global edge network — fast, secure by default, and built to scale, with no separate hosting bill to manage yourself.

07How exactly is Claude used in development?

Claude is used throughout the build — from architecture and code to content and on-page SEO/GEO structure — with every result reviewed and refined by hand rather than shipped as raw AI output. The same approach powers the chatbots and automations built for clients.

08What does a website from Visseo cost?

Visseo offers low-cost websites, since AI-assisted development cuts the hours a traditional build takes. Every project gets a tailored quote based on scope — get in touch for a free estimate.
