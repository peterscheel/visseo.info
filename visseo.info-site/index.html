FAQ

Questions, answered.

01What does Visseo do?

Visseo is a web studio for SEO and GEO. We handcraft websites that are optimized for search and AI search from day one, and add AI consulting, branding and Cloudflare-hosted infrastructure for businesses across BeNeLux and Germany.

02What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing content and structure so AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can find, understand and cite it — alongside traditional SEO for search engines like Google.

03What role does AI play in your websites?

Every site is designed, built and reviewed by hand. AI tools support the routine groundwork, which is how we keep prices fair without compromising on quality. Hosting runs on Cloudflare's global edge network.

04Which regions does Visseo serve?

Visseo works with businesses across BeNeLux (Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg) and Germany.

05Who is behind Visseo?

Visseo is run by Peter Scheel, who has worked hands-on on production websites since being introduced to web design, SEO and hosting by his brother's agency. Visseo grew out of that experience as a side project in 2022.

06Where are Visseo websites hosted?

On Cloudflare's global edge network — fast, secure by default, and built to scale, with no separate hosting bill to manage yourself.

07How exactly is Claude used in development?

Claude assists with groundwork such as code scaffolding, first drafts and on-page SEO/GEO structure. Every result is then reviewed, refined and signed off by hand. The time saved goes straight into fairer prices. The same approach powers the chatbots and automations we build for clients.

08What does a website from Visseo cost?

Websites from Visseo are priced fairly: efficient tooling shortens the build without changing the craftsmanship. Every project receives a quote tailored to its scope — get in touch for a free estimate.
