This directory contains subsetted, self-hosted builds of three Google Fonts
typefaces used by visseo.info: Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, and Inter.

All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, by
their respective copyright holders (Florian Karsten / Colophon Foundry for
Space Grotesk, the JetBrains Mono contributors, and the Inter contributors
led by Rasmus Andersson). The original, unmodified license texts and source
files are available at:

  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jetbrainsmono
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter

The .woff2 files here are variable-font builds, subsetted to Latin/Latin-1
characters (covering English, German and Dutch) and re-encoded from the
same upstream sources. No glyph outlines were altered.
