Mon02Nov

Hidden craft brews at Bionic Brew (indie favourite in Nantou). Early night – tomorrow morning train from Futian Station (≈30 min from the hotel).

Tue03Nov

Morning bullet train Futian → West Kowloon (≈15 min), taxi/MTR to Tai Hang. Afternoon & evening around Tai Hang / Causeway Bay.

Tai Hang’s little eateries around Wun Sha Street, OR Tung Po Kitchen (North Point, 10 min – drink beer out of bowls!)

Outdoor / Hike

🥾 Hike: Jardine’s Lookout / Mount Butler (trailhead reachable on foot from Tai Hang, harbour views)

Culture & Sights

Victoria Park and the small Lin Fa Kung temple in Tai Hang

Evening

Causeway Bay night streets, then a beer in one of Tai Hang’s small bars

Wed04Nov

🎂 Birthday: Dragon’s Back in the morning (east, ≈20 min), afternoon in Admiralty/Central (HK Park, Peak Tram), dinner at Pacific Place – all next to each other.

Thu05Nov

Fri06Nov

Evening Pearl River cruise under the lit Canton Tower

Stroll the tree-lined alleys of Dongshankou art district and eat herbal desserts at a tiny stall there

Tue10Nov

Classic Cantonese roast meats (char siu & roast goose) around Canton Tower / Haizhu

Specialty coffee in the converted textile factory of TIT Creative Park, next to Canton Tower

Local craft beers at the Pearl River Party Pier art zone (Pazhou, near the hotel)

Wed11Nov

🚄 Morning high-speed train Guangzhou South → Zhangjiajie West (≈5–6 h, usually via Changsha). Taxi ≈40–50 min to Pandora Hilltop Hotel in the Wulingyuan / park area – ask the hotel how to get there with luggage (park ticket / pick-up?).

Sanxiaguo 三下锅 (Zhangjiajie's signature spicy "three-in-one" dry pot) in Wulingyuan

Outdoor / Hike

Evening walk through Wulingyuan town along the Suoxi River

Culture & Sights

Buy park tickets (valid several days) and plan tomorrow’s route

Evening

Gear check, buy cheap bamboo walking poles and prep packs for the early start

Thu12Nov
Zhangjiajie🏨 Pandora Hilltop Hotel

Full day National Forest Park, starting right from the hotel area (Bailong Elevator ≈10 min away): Bailong Elevator, Yuanjiajie (Avatar mountains), Tianzi Mountain.

Food

Tujia Style Smoked Pork Pot (Savory, intense local ethnic minority specialty dish)

Outdoor / Hike

Hike (ANTI-CROWD PATH): At the Wulingyuan gate at opening (~07:00), shuttle bus straight to Bailong Elevator before the tour groups, Yuanjiajie first, then along to Tianzi Mountain.

Culture & Sights

Reddit Tip: If the tour groups turn left, you turn right. Keep your hands free to dodge wild monkeys.

Evening

Ride the Tianzi Mountain cableway down in the late afternoon (before closing) for crowd-free views, back to Wulingyuan.

Fri13Nov
Zhangjiajie🏨 Pandora Hilltop Hotel

Tianmen Mountain (cableway starts in Zhangjiajie city, ≈45 min from the hotel): Heaven's Gate, glass skywalks.

Food

Xiangxi Wild Mushroom Stew (Comforting claypot with forest-foraged mushrooms)

Outdoor / Hike

Hike: Catch one of the first cableways up Tianmen Mountain (check opening time the day before) to walk the Glass Skywalks before the groups arrive.

Culture & Sights

Climb the 999 steps up to Heaven's Gate (Tianmen Cave) instead of taking the escalators.

Evening

Back in Wulingyuan: ice-cold local beers at a quiet guesthouse away from the neon centre.

Wed18Nov
🚄 Chongqing → Chengdu🏨 SSAW Garden Hotel Chunxi Road

Thu19Nov

Panda Base early in the morning (north), Wenshu Monastery on the way back, then People's Park & Kuanzhai Alley (next to each other) in the afternoon.

Evening

Kuanzhai Alley 宽窄巷子 lit up in the evening (≈1.5 km from People's Park), then metro Line 2 home

Fri20Nov

Mountain Village Farmhouse (Smoked bacon, wild bamboo shoots & forest herbs) at the foot of Qingcheng

Back in Chengdu: evening stroll through Taikoo Li 太古里 next to the hotel

Sat21Nov
🚄 Chengdu → Chongqing🏨 Aranciel High-Rise River View Hotel (Jiefangbei / Hongyadong)

Last morning around Chunxi Road, check out (Line 2 to Chengdu East ≈20 min). 🚄 Midday train Chengdu East → Chongqing (≈1–1.5 h). Check in at Jiefangbei, evening walk across the river and back.

Food

Dinner: Chongqing grilled fish 烤鱼 around Jiefangbei

Final tea in the courtyard teahouse of Daci Temple 大慈寺 (inside Taikoo Li, ≈5 min walk) before the train

Culture & Sights

Walk across Qiansimen Bridge 千厮门大桥 to Jiangbeizui for the classic view back at the lit-up Hongyadong

Evening

Night view from your high-rise river-view room, or the Raffles City "Crystal" sky bridge at Chaotianmen (≈10 min walk)

Sun22Nov
Chongqing🏨 Aranciel High-Rise River View Hotel (Jiefangbei / Hongyadong)

Spicy street food in Guanyinqiao's alleyway basements

🍻 The Ninth Street 九街 (Guanyinqiao bar & nightlife street), then back to the hotel
